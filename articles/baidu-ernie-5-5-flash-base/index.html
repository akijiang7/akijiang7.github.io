Baidu releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base weights, with territorial and commercial conditions

Key points

  • US use is within the licensed territory, but global use is not. The custom license excludes the European Union, the United Kingdom and South Korea. Its territorial restrictions cover the model, derivatives and outputs, including non-commercial use. A US company or US-hosted server does not by itself establish permission to serve every market. See Sections 1.11, 2 and 5.3 of the license reviewed.
  • Commercial use comes with conditions. At or below US$50 million in related-product or service revenue over the preceding 12 consecutive months, commercial use is free of model-license charges but requires a one-time notice before first use and prominent Powered by ERNIE attribution. Above that threshold, prior written commercial authorization is required. Related revenue is aggregated across entities under common control; these rules are not limited to inference-API businesses.
  • Baidu's official announcement is dated November 5, 2026. ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base weights are available on Hugging Face. Earlier repository uploads are not used here as evidence of the public release date.
  • This is a base checkpoint for further training, not a post-trained chat assistant. Baidu reports 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion activated per token and a 33.5-trillion-token pre-training corpus. It describes a training context of up to 128K tokens, with support for up to 1M during fine-tuning and inference.

What was released

ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base is a text-only mixture-of-experts model taken at the end of pre-training, before mid-training or post-training. Baidu positions it as a starting point for domain adaptation, instruction tuning and reinforcement-learning research, rather than a finished conversational product.

The official announcement lists FP8 and BF16 weight releases. We confirmed that the primary Hugging Face repository contains 128 safetensors weight shards, not just a model card. Baidu also provides architecture and evaluation descriptions. The model figures and capabilities cited here are Baidu's reports; MoxBench has not independently evaluated this checkpoint.

Recent repository activity should not be confused with a second weight release. The November 5 updates include model-card edits and a tokenizer configuration correction that explicitly sets its maximum length to 1,048,576. That configuration change is not evidence of new weights or a newly demonstrated capability.

The commercial conditions are not an MIT-style permission

The license at the reviewed repository revision defines the following conditions:

  • Revenue scope: use the preceding rolling 12 months, not simply a calendar year. Count the full revenue of qualifying products or services that use, incorporate or rely on the model or its derivatives, even if those products also use other models. Aggregate that related revenue across entities under common control. It is not just revenue attributed to ERNIE or to model-API sales. See Sections 1.7 and 4.
  • At or below US$50 million: send one notice for the same operator and model version before its first commercial use. Section 4.2 allows qualifying use to proceed after the notice is sent without waiting for a response or approval. Exactly US$50 million falls in this category; the other license obligations still apply.
  • Above US$50 million: obtain separate written authorization before starting above-threshold commercial use. If revenue will cross the threshold during use, contact Baidu in advance and obtain authorization before crossing it. A notice, application or pending negotiation is not that authorization. See Section 4.3.
  • Attribution: all commercial use must prominently display Powered by ERNIE. Section 3.2 permits an appropriate accessible interface, website, about page or product documentation; API and backend services can use their developer documentation, and internal business use can use internal documentation. Written commercial authorization does not remove this requirement unless its terms expressly change it.
  • Territory and other obligations: neither non-commercial status nor a low revenue level removes the territorial restrictions. Redistribution obligations, acceptable-use rules and the agreement's other restrictions also remain applicable. These bullets are not an exhaustive substitute for the license.

What this means for US developers

This is a base checkpoint intended for further training, not a ready-to-use chat assistant. We describe it as a base model with conditionally available weights, not as an open-source release.

US teams need to assess the actual locations of team access, service delivery and output display, not just incorporation or hosting. Uses involving the excluded regions should not be assumed authorized under this license; cross-border arrangements need separate clarification or authorization. Commercial planning also needs the related-product revenue calculation, notice or written authorization, and attribution requirements described above.

This is a factual summary of the reviewed documents, not legal advice or a deployment recommendation.
