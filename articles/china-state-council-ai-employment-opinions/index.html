China's State Council orders job-risk reviews for AI deployments: full English translation of Guobanfa [2026] No. 29

Read the full text ↓

Key points

  • On October 23, 2026, the General Office of China's State Council published 18 measures on adapting to AI while keeping employment stable (Guobanfa [2026] No. 29, dated October 16). The text is available only in Chinese.
  • Job-risk reviews: AI application projects in market-access areas that could cause large job losses must get an employment-risk assessment during project review. Companies are encouraged to set up an "unemployment buffer period" and to tell affected workers in advance before rolling out AI at scale.
  • Open source as a jobs tool: the document repeatedly backs open source. It calls for "high-level AI open-source communities", a batch of high-quality open-source projects, and more company investment in public-interest AI and open-source communities.
  • AI in hiring: it calls for regulating AI use in HR and for cracking down on algorithm-driven employment discrimination, fake job postings and privacy violations.
  • Monitoring: AI-driven job losses are added to China's existing system for preventing large-scale unemployment, backed by new monitoring models and AI employment research centers.

Issuing authority: General Office of the State Council · Document number: Guobanfa [2026] No. 29 · Date written: October 16, 2026 · Date published: October 23, 2026

Opinions of the General Office of the State Council on Adapting to the Development of Artificial Intelligence Technology and Helping Build an Employment-Friendly Development Model

To the people's governments of all provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and all ministries, commissions and agencies directly under the State Council:

Employment is the most basic component of people's livelihood. To implement the decisions and arrangements of the Party Central Committee and the State Council on promoting high-quality and full employment, to actively adapt to the development of artificial intelligence technology, and to help build an employment-friendly development model, the following opinions are put forward with the approval of the State Council.

I. General requirements

Guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, fully implementing the guiding principles of the 20th National Congress of the Communist Party of China and of all plenary sessions of the 20th Central Committee, we will adhere to a people-centered development philosophy, closely combine investment in physical assets with investment in people, coordinate development and security, and adhere to an employment-first orientation. Taking AI-enabled high-quality development as the opportunity, promoting high-quality and full employment by adapting to technological development as the goal, and building an employment-friendly development model as the direction, we will actively strengthen the positive effects of AI in creating employment opportunities, empowering traditional jobs, improving the quality of employment and stimulating entrepreneurial vitality; effectively mitigate the job-displacement impact of AI applications; and strive for inclusive development in which technological progress and industrial upgrading are coordinated with employment promotion and improvement of people's livelihoods.

II. Strengthen the job-creation effect of artificial intelligence

(1) Expand new space for AI employment in emerging fields. Thoroughly implement the "AI Plus" initiative; encourage eastern regions to develop core AI industries, and guide central and western regions to take on upstream and downstream industries such as data annotation and smart-terminal manufacturing in light of local conditions, so as to accelerate the formation of new growth points for employment in the AI field. Accelerate the cultivation of AI-native enterprises and specialized, sophisticated "little giant" enterprises that produce novel and unique products; in accordance with regulations, include qualifying enterprises in the scope of special loans supporting enterprises in stabilizing and expanding jobs, creating more new jobs and opportunities.

(2) Foster new momentum for small, medium and micro enterprises to stabilize and expand jobs. Implement an AI SME entrepreneurship support program, and use the open-source ecosystem to promote innovation and entrepreneurship by SMEs. Strengthen the supply of public services for digital transformation; encourage the development of "small, fast, light and precise" digital products and solutions; ensure that small, medium and micro enterprises and individual businesses have equal access, in accordance with the law, to intelligent factors of production such as data and algorithms; support small, medium and micro enterprises in exploring low-cost, lightweight paths to intelligent upgrading; further lower the threshold for AI applications; and enhance their capacity to absorb employment.

(3) Create new models of employment and entrepreneurship in the AI field. Make good use of government investment funds such as the National Venture Capital Guidance Fund and the National AI Industry Investment Fund to support, through market-based approaches, the innovative development of enterprises in the AI field. Build high-level AI open-source communities and deploy a batch of high-quality open-source projects, generating the effect of entrepreneurship driving employment. Promote the models of "scientific and technological achievements + entrepreneurship", "industrial development + entrepreneurship", "vocational skills + entrepreneurship" and "livelihood needs + entrepreneurship"; for entrepreneurial projects centered on innovation in AI application scenarios and the transfer of technological achievements, implement entrepreneurship support policies and provide incubation services.

(4) Accelerate the development of new occupations in the AI field. Focusing on the cultivation and large-scale application of AI scenarios, accelerate the development of new occupations and the release of new occupational standards, and expand demand for new occupations and job types such as digital managers, AI engineering and technical personnel, and AI trainers. Encourage localities, in light of the needs of AI industry development, to add relevant specialties for professional-title evaluation and to promptly carry out AI-related vocational skill level certification.

III. Build an employment-friendly AI upgrading path

(5) Tap the employment potential of AI in traditional sectors. Leverage AI's role in helping transform and upgrade traditional industries; guide the better adoption of human–machine collaboration models in the intelligent development of all industrial factors and the digital-intelligent transformation and upgrading of agriculture; and tap digital-intelligent employment opportunities in traditional sectors. Accelerate the building of new AI-driven service models; by sector, promote the extension of producer services toward specialization and the high end of the value chain; promote high-quality, diversified and convenient development of consumer services; expand new service-consumption scenarios such as experiential and personalized consumption; and expand the employment capacity of the service sector.

(6) Mitigate the job-displacement impact of AI applications. Leverage AI's role in improving the employment environment, raising occupational safety and improving work quality and efficiency, and give priority to promoting its application in positions with labor shortages or hazardous environments. Prudently manage the scope, intensity and pace of AI application in labor-intensive industries, minimizing the impact on workers as far as possible. Encourage enterprises to explore establishing an unemployment buffer-period system and, in the course of large-scale rollout of AI, to notify affected workers in advance and provide support for transition and redeployment.

(7) Establish an employment-risk assessment mechanism for AI applications. Comprehensively assess the impact of AI applications on employment, and guide innovation resources toward areas with greater potential to create jobs. For AI application scenario projects in market-access fields that may cause a relatively large employment shock, carry out an employment-risk assessment at the same time as project review, and the relevant departments shall make overall arrangements for transition and redeployment support. For AI application scenario projects in other fields, carry out employment-risk assessments as appropriate to actual conditions.

IV. Improve workers' digital literacy and employability

(8) Jointly cultivate innovative AI talent. Encourage regular higher-education institutions and vocational schools (including technical schools) to accelerate the dynamic optimization of disciplines and majors, make extraordinary arrangements for disciplines and majors urgently needed in the AI field, strengthen relevant interdisciplinary programs, add more AI "micro-majors", implement programs to train interdisciplinary talent, and cultivate more interdisciplinary AI talent. Strengthen joint training by schools and enterprises, and support leading science and technology enterprises in participating in curriculum development, teacher training and the upgrading of teaching facilities. Solidly advance AI education at all levels of schooling, promote student internships in enterprises and teaching by enterprise engineers in schools, and cultivate students' exploratory and creative thinking.

(9) Implement a special AI training program. Thoroughly implement the "Skills Light Up the Future" training initiative. When localities carry out project-based and order-based digital technology skills training, they should closely follow market demand in the AI field, and encourage young people such as college graduates, migrant workers and unemployed persons to participate in employment and entrepreneurship training programs in the AI field. Encourage localities to actively carry out AI-related skills training relying on public training bases, enterprise training bases and high-skilled talent training bases. Advance general AI education for the whole of society, and launch more free public courses relying on the National Smart Education Public Service Platform, the online learning platform for industrial and information technology skilled talent, continuing-education online platforms and others. Encourage the active inclusion of AI-related skills competitions in existing vocational skills contests.

V. Promote AI-enabled high-quality employment policy services

(10) Use AI to help implement employment policies. Explore building an intelligent identification mechanism for policy implementation, analyze the characteristics of service recipients, push employment policies intelligently, and improve the quality and efficiency of "policies finding people". Strengthen the application of AI in employment policy evaluation, fund management, publicity and interpretation, and consultation.

(11) Raise the level of intelligence in employment services. Upgrade and improve the national employment information resource database, the national college-student employment service platform and the "China Employment" public service platform, and strengthen data aggregation, intelligent analysis and precise services. Advance the construction of national AI application pilot bases, make overall use of existing resources to explore the intelligent upgrading of grassroots public employment service networks, and deepen the application of AI in fields such as mediation and arbitration of labor and personnel disputes. Promote AI-enabled human-resources services, and encourage business entities to innovate and optimize new products and models such as job-search and recruitment large models, intelligent interviews and virtual-reality training.

(12) Increase support for transition and redeployment. In accordance with regulations, provide employment assistance to qualifying workers affected by job displacement from AI applications, offering targeted career guidance, skills training, job referrals, entrepreneurship support and other services. In accordance with regulations, pay unemployment-insurance benefits to eligible insured unemployed persons promptly and in full.

VI. Improve systems for protecting workers' rights and interests and the social-security system

(13) Optimize worker-rights protection systems adapted to human–machine collaboration. Strengthen research on protecting workers' basic rights and interests in light of new features of the social division of labor such as human–machine collaboration; improve consultation and coordination mechanisms for workers' rights and interests; and urge enterprises to protect, in accordance with the law, workers' lawful rights and interests such as receiving remuneration, rest and leave, and occupational safety and health protection. Regulate the application of AI in human-resources management, and govern in accordance with the law illegal and non-compliant acts such as employment discrimination, false recruitment, and infringement of privacy rights and rest rights caused by the abuse of algorithms.

(14) Improve social-security systems for workers in flexible employment and new forms of employment. Adapting to the development of AI technology, enhance the flexibility and inclusiveness of the social-security system; encourage and support persons in flexible employment and new forms of employment to participate in basic pension insurance for urban employees; and expand pilots of occupational-injury protection for workers in new forms of employment. Make active use of the dividends of AI development to better safeguard employment and improve people's livelihoods.

(15) Improve income-distribution mechanisms adapted to the development of AI technology. Guide enterprises to establish and improve salary-distribution systems suited to the characteristics of personnel working in the AI field; improve wage-distribution incentive mechanisms for high-level talent oriented toward increasing the value of knowledge; and implement the classified reference guidelines on minimum wages for skilled talent. Cultivate and develop innovative forms of work such as intelligent agents; encourage enterprises to increase investment in public-interest AI technologies, services and open-source communities; help workers expand the boundaries of their abilities and raise the value of their labor; and enable more workers to actively participate in and benefit from technological progress.

VII. Improve the system for monitoring, early warning and response to employment risks from AI applications

(16) Strengthen monitoring and early warning of employment risks from AI applications. Strengthen cross-departmental sharing of data on AI-related industries, employment and other matters, and jointly build regional and sector-specific intelligent models for monitoring and analyzing employment data. Strengthen advance research and forecasting of employment risks from AI applications, and make the monitoring and early warning of employment risks more sensitive.

(17) Strengthen research on the employment impact of AI applications. Carry out special surveys on the employment impact of AI applications, and continuously track the penetration of AI applications, job creation and displacement, changes in skill demand and other conditions. Relying on existing resources such as universities and research institutions, establish a number of AI employment research centers. Properly implement research projects such as those of the National Social Science Fund, and conduct forward-looking research on the impact of AI technology development on employment and income distribution.

(18) Improve mechanisms for responding to employment risks from AI applications. Include employment risks from AI applications within the scope of the mechanism for preventing and defusing large-scale unemployment risks, and formulate risk-response plans in an integrated manner. Strengthen joint responses to labor-relations risks related to AI, and do a good job of consultation, assessment and handling of risks.

VIII. Strengthen organization and implementation

Uphold the Party's leadership throughout every aspect and the whole process of the work of adapting to AI technology development and promoting employment and entrepreneurship. All regions and relevant departments shall effectively implement these opinions in light of actual conditions, further strengthen the policy orientation of giving priority to employment, take the employment impact of AI applications into overall consideration, and accelerate the building of an employment-friendly development model. Human resources and social security departments and development and reform departments at all levels shall strengthen overall coordination, promote the implementation of all policy measures, strengthen publicity and guidance, and foster a favorable social atmosphere for promoting employment and entrepreneurship. Major matters shall be reported to the Party Central Committee and the State Council for instructions in a timely manner and in accordance with procedures.

General Office of the State Council

October 16, 2026

What this means for US developers

This is a policy document, not a law with penalties, but it tells companies selling AI products in China what to expect. The main operational change is in Measure 7. AI deployments in market-access areas that could cause large job losses will now get an employment-risk assessment when the project is reviewed. Measure 6 adds advance notice and an "unemployment buffer period" for workers affected by large AI rollouts. If you sell automation, agent or HR-tech products to Chinese enterprises, expect customers to ask for job-impact information during procurement. Measure 13 points to tighter enforcement against algorithmic hiring discrimination, fake job postings and privacy violations. That matters for recruitment and AI interview tools, which Measure 11 also says it wants to encourage. For the open-model ecosystem, the signal is support: the State Council names AI open-source communities and open-source projects as tools for creating jobs and asks companies to invest in them. Expect Chinese labs to keep releasing open weights, and local governments to fund open-source projects.
