Apple Intelligence is coming to China with Alibaba's Qwen in Siri: developer beta Nov 4, release with iOS 27.3 in December

Key points

  • Apple announced on October 31, 2026, in a Chinese-language press release on its China newsroom, that the first Apple Intelligence features are coming to iPhone users in mainland China. They go into testing with the latest iOS developer beta from November 4, and Apple says they will reach Chinese users when iOS 27.3 is officially released in December.
  • Apple says it is partnering with Alibaba to add support for Alibaba's Qwen (千问) to some Apple Intelligence experiences, namely Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence, including text and image understanding. The original sentence reads: "让中国用户能直接在 Siri、写作工具以及视觉智能中使用阿里巴巴的千问，包括文本与图像理解相关功能".
  • Apple says Apple Intelligence itself runs on generative models Apple built. Qwen is an added option in specific places.
  • The update does not include Siri AI or the other next-generation Apple Intelligence features Apple announced at WWDC 2026.
  • Alibaba has not issued its own official announcement, investor-relations release or HKEX filing about the partnership as of November 2, 2026. Alibaba's IR and HKEX filings pages show nothing related; the latest filing there is dated October 14.

What happened

On October 31, 2026, Apple published a press release on apple.com.cn titled "Apple 智能即将登陆中国" ("Apple Intelligence is coming to China"). It says the first wave of Apple Intelligence features is coming to China and names Alibaba as a partner. In Apple's words (our translation), the deal adds Qwen support "to some Apple Intelligence experiences, letting users in China use Alibaba's Qwen directly in Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence."

Apple says support for other platforms will follow "after obtaining the necessary approvals" (our translation). It does not say which approvals or which platforms.

We found no English version on Apple's global newsroom. The Oct 31 release isn't in the apple.com/newsroom RSS feed, and the same URL path on apple.com returns 404. The Chinese page is the only primary source.

Key details

Timeline

  • November 4, 2026: testing begins with the latest iOS developer beta.
  • December 2026: release to users in China with iOS 27.3.

Where Qwen appears

  • Siri: for certain requests Siri can draw on Qwen's knowledge. Users can also ask questions about photos and documents, including PDFs and presentations.
  • Writing Tools: a Compose (创作) feature lets Qwen write original content from scratch, anywhere Writing Tools work across the system.
  • Visual Intelligence: Apple lists it among the Qwen-supported experiences.
  • Sign-in: users reach Qwen inside Apple Intelligence by signing in to a Qwen account or with Sign in with Apple.

Other Apple Intelligence features listed for China

  • Live Translation in Messages, FaceTime and Phone. It also works for in-person conversations with supported AirPods: AirPods 5, AirPods 4 with ANC, AirPods Pro 2 and later, or AirPods Max 2.
  • Writing Tools: rewrite, proofread and summarize.
  • Visual Intelligence through the camera.
  • Clean Up in Photos.
  • Genmoji (智绘表情).
  • Natural-language search in Photos, and Memory movies created from a description.
  • Priority Messages in Mail, Mail summaries, and Smart Reply in Mail and Messages.
  • Notification summaries, Priority Notifications, and the Reduce Interruptions Focus.
  • Summaries of audio transcriptions in Notes and of call transcripts.
  • Typing to Siri, with better handling of halting speech and follow-up context.

Requirements Apple states

  • Devices: iPhone 16 and later, iPhone Duo, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and iPhone Air.
  • Language: Siri and device language set to English or Chinese (Simplified).
  • Apple notes that some features may be available only in some languages or regions.

Alibaba's side

As of November 2, 2026, Alibaba has published no official announcement, IR release or HKEX filing on the partnership. Xinhua, China's state news agency, has reported comments attributed to Alibaba. That is secondary coverage, and we have not verified those comments against an Alibaba primary source.

What this means for US developers

  • Testing: if you ship iOS apps in China, test against the developer beta from November 4. Many Apple Intelligence features in this list surface system-wide, through Writing Tools, notifications and Visual Intelligence.
  • Built-in assistant: Chinese users get Alibaba's Qwen as the assistant inside Siri and Writing Tools, with sign-in through a Qwen account or Sign in with Apple. Expect users in China to arrive with Qwen-assisted text and photo or document Q&A built into the OS.
  • Global builds: nothing in Apple's release changes Apple Intelligence outside China, and it describes no new developer API.
  • Before you plan around it: the release leaves open which approvals are still pending for "other platforms". Wait for Alibaba's own disclosure and Apple's iOS 27.3 release notes.
