Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian) October update: 4 new models, and four Qwen3.5 model IDs retire Jan 19, 2027

Key points

  • Alibaba Cloud's Bailian (Model Studio) monthly product report for October 2026, posted November 2 on the Alibaba Cloud Developer Community, lists four new models: Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime-Flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo and Qwen-Image-2.1-Pro.
  • Alibaba Cloud's official service notice (published Oct 19, 2026) says Model Studio will retire four legacy Qwen3.5 model IDs on January 19, 2027 at 10:00 (UTC+8), a time the notice says is "subject to the actual change time" (plan your migration well before then): qwen3.5-plus, qwen3.5-plus-2026-02-15, qwen3.5-plus-2026-04-20 and qwen3.5-397b-a17b. The recommended replacement for all four is qwen3.8-flash.
  • New purchases of these models stop on November 18, 2026 (end of marketing). After January 19, calls to them will fail.
  • Alibaba Cloud's official Model Studio model-deprecation documentation confirms the January 19, 2027 batch and links the same notice.

What happened

The monthly report was posted on Alibaba Cloud's Developer Community in the Bailian section, under the account "bailiantest1". The page carries the community's standard notice that content belongs to its author, so we treat it as a lead. Every retirement detail below is confirmed against Alibaba Cloud's own service notice and deprecation documentation.

According to the report, the new models cover real-time speech synthesis (Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime-Flash), text generation (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, which the report describes as "supplied directly by Alibaba" and usable with Token Plan credits), and text-to-image generation and image editing (Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo and Qwen-Image-2.1-Pro). The report gives no pricing or specifications for them.

  • Nov 18, 2026 (EOM): you can no longer activate these models or buy new resource packs for them.
  • Jan 19, 2027: inference stops completely. In the notice's words, applications still calling the models "will fail to receive responses".
  • Plans and refunds: the same models are also retired from the Coding Plan and Token Plan. Holders of prepaid packages can request a pro-rata refund through a support ticket.
  • No further updates promised: the notice says its replacement recommendations will not be updated as new models launch, and it advises checking the Model Gallery.

Other items in the monthly report:

  • 69 new templates in the app marketplace and 25 new MCP services.
  • The Smart Routing entry has been separated from Dedicated Deployment, with its own permission taking effect Nov 3, 2026.
  • The "Quark web search component" will be retired on April 12, 2027, in favor of the Web Search and Enhanced Web Search MCP tools.
  • A 30%-off Wan3.0-Video pay-as-you-go promotion is extended to Nov 30.

What this means for US developers

If you call qwen3.5-plus, any dated qwen3.5-plus-* snapshot or qwen3.5-397b-a17b through Model Studio's API, including the international (Singapore) endpoint, plan a migration before January 19, 2027. Treat November 18 as the last day to buy new capacity. The suggested replacement, qwen3.8-flash, is listed at much lower prices, but it is a different model tier ("flash" rather than "plus"), so run your own evals before switching. Pinning to a dated snapshot does not protect you here: two dated IDs are on the list. Under Alibaba Cloud's documented policy, stable models get at least three months' notice before end of service. Watch the deprecation page and route new work to models that are not on it.
