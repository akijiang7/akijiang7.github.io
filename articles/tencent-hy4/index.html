Tencent launches Hy4, with visual understanding and computer use; weights promised 'in the coming days'

Key points

  • On November 4, 2026, Tencent announced Hy4, its latest-generation large language model. Tencent describes it as now ranking "among the world's leading open-source AI models". That characterization is Tencent's.
  • Tencent says it will open-source the weights soon; currently only the August Hy4-preview is on HF. The press release says Tencent Hy "plans to release model weights and system cards in the coming days." As of Nov 5, 2026, Tencent's Hugging Face organization has only tencent/Hy4-preview and tencent/Hy4-preview-FP8 (both Apache-2.0, created Aug 27, 2026). We found no tencent/Hy4 repository.
  • New compared with the preview, according to Tencent: visual understanding (charts, fine-grained image processing, visual math reasoning) and computer use across command-line and graphical interfaces. Tencent also cites better instruction following, long-context handling and long-horizon agentic tasks, plus training on Blender and CAD.
  • Pricing (Tencent): "approximately US$0.90 per million input tokens, US$2.70 per million output tokens and US$0.045 per million cached input tokens", the same as Hy4 Preview. Hy4 is 50% off on Tencent Cloud TokenHub through Dec 31, 2026.
  • OpenRouter price differs: OpenRouter's model API lists tencent/hy4 at US$0.417 per million input tokens, US$1.2505 per million output tokens and US$0.021 per million cached input tokens (checked Nov 5, 2026).

Details from the press release

  • Availability: Tencent Cloud TokenHub, WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, WeCom's AI assistant, ima and other Tencent products. The API is also offered through third parties including OpenRouter, Hermes and OpenCode. WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy are free from Nov 4 to 15.
  • Tencent-reported evaluations (not independently verified):
  • On WorkBuddy's evaluation set, Hy4 averaged 83.18 with a 96.15% pass rate and was "first place in nine out of twelve scenarios within the mainland Chinese market".
  • In a blind finance evaluation by Tencent's own team and Tencent Financial Technology (213 financial professionals, 579 tasks), Tencent reports Hy4 at 3.04 vs. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 at 2.97 and Kimi K3.1 Pro and GLM-5.3 at 2.68 each.
  • Tencent reports the hallucination rate on internal evaluations fell from 12.95% (Hy4 Preview) to 7.2%.
  • It reports gains of 19.1% on GameDev and 9.8% on GameLogic benchmarks over the preview.
  • Safety: Tencent says offensive exploitation material was excluded from training. Its Xuanwu Lab and Keen Security Lab found "no significant concerns" about offensive cyber capability in the areas they evaluated, and Tencent says biological experimental-design capability is "more limited compared to frontier models."
  • Also announced: two open-source science models built with Hy4 and the Hyra 2.0 harness (HyCell for single-cell representations, HyPDE for physics fields). A cheaper Hy4 Flash is "scheduled for release soon".

Note: the press release is copyrighted. We summarize it and do not reproduce it. Read the original.

What this means for US developers

You can call Hy4 today through OpenRouter. Its listed per-token price is currently below the figure in Tencent's press release, so check the provider page before you budget. You cannot self-host Hy4 yet. If you need open weights now, the August tencent/Hy4-preview is Apache-2.0, but it lacks the vision and computer-use features Tencent describes for Hy4. Wait for the actual Hy4 repository and its license before planning on-prem use: the preview's Apache-2.0 terms may not carry over. All benchmark claims above are Tencent's own. Treat them as vendor claims until third-party evaluations appear.
